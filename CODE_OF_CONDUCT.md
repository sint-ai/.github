# Code of Conduct

We want SINT repositories to stay respectful, practical, and high-signal.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Share evidence and context instead of blame.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment or personal attacks
- spam or deliberate disruption
- doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- public disclosure of vulnerabilities or secrets

Maintainers may moderate content that violates these standards.
